## Full Description (4000 characters limit)
The ultimate study companion for the Japanese Real Estate Transaction Specialist (Takken / 宅建士) National Examination, taken by over 200,000 candidates annually!

"Takken Drill" helps you master the four core examination subjects (Civil Law & Rights, Real Estate Brokerage Act, Legal Restrictions, and Taxes/Appraisal) through 30 carefully curated, high-yield exam questions, interactive simulators, and a quick-reference cheat sheet.

Rather than plain rote memorization, this app breaks down complex legal relationships with ASCII party interaction diagrams and provides insider "Exam Tips" revealing the tricky traps commonly set by exam examiners.

### ⚡ Key Features

1. **30 High-Yield Exam Questions Across 4 Core Domains**
   - **Civil Law & Rights (権利関係)**: Fraud, legal capacity, unauthorized agency, double sales, statutory surface rights, earnest money cancellation, fixed-term leases.
   - **Real Estate Brokerage Act (宅建業法)**: 5-year disqualification rules, mandatory licensed agents (1-in-5 rule), 3 mediation contract types, Article 35 vs 37 comparison, commission calculation formula, cooling-off rules, 20% damages cap.
   - **Legal Restrictions (法令上の制限)**: 13 zoning districts, development permission scales, Section 2 road setbacks, building coverage and FAR bonuses, National Land Use Planning Act post-facto filing, Agricultural Land Act Articles 3, 4, 5.
   - **Taxes & Housing Finance (税・その他)**: Real estate acquisition tax, fixed asset property tax, stamp tax, Japan Housing Finance Agency operations, 80-meter walking distance advertising rules.

2. **ASCII Party Relationship Diagrams & In-Depth Exam Tips**
   - ASCII visual diagrams for every question to simplify complex multi-party interactions (Buyer A, Seller B, Bad-faith C, Transferee D).
   - Thorough explanation for every choice with statutory references and Supreme Court precedents.
   - Targeted "Exam Tips" showing you exactly how exam writers construct trick questions.

3. **⚡ Interactive Legal Simulators**
   - **Double Sale & Bad Faith (二重譲渡と登記)**: Step-by-step simulation of competing property buyers, registration priority, malicious bad-faith exceptions, and bona fide third-party transferees.
   - **Article 35 (Statement) vs Article 37 (Contract)**: Interactive side-by-side comparison of delivery timing, recipients, oral explanation duties, and mandatory price clauses.
   - **Cooling-Off Location Verdict**: Toggle locations (hotel lobby, registered branch office, buyer-requested home visit, unsolicited visit) to verify legal cooling-off eligibility instantly.

4. **Essential Exam Cheat Sheet (24 Quick-Reference Items)**
   - Brokerage fee quick calculation formula (Price x 3% + 60,000 yen + 10% tax).
   - Mediation contract comparison matrix (Exclusive, Full Exclusive, General).
   - Earnest money protection thresholds (5% uncompleted, 10% completed).
   - Development permission acreage limits.
   - One-tap copy to clipboard for rapid revision.

5. **Study Progress Tracking & Bookmark Review**
   - Real-time subject mastery progress and accuracy tracking.
   - Bookmark challenging questions for quick targeted review before exam day.

6. **100% Offline & Complete Privacy**
   - Works fully offline—study seamlessly during subway commutes or flights.
   - Zero login required, no external server tracking. Full English and Japanese support.
